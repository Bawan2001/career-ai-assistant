# Remote Work & International Tech Job Hunting Strategy

## Core Principles
1. **Asynchronous Communication**: Emphasize clear written communication, documentation habits, and self-management.
2. **Global Remote Platforms**: Use platforms like Wellfound (AngelList), RemoteOK, Turing, WeWorkRemotely, LinkedIn Jobs filtered by Remote.
3. **Time Zone Alignment**: Highlight experience working across overlapping time zones (EST, UTC, SGT).
