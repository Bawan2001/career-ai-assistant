# Behavioral Interview Strategy: The STAR Technique

## The STAR Method Structure
- **Situation**: Contextualize the challenge or project background (15% of answer).
- **Task**: Explain your specific responsibility or role in addressing the problem (15% of answer).
- **Action**: Describe the precise technical steps, collaboration, and decisions YOU made (50% of answer).
- **Result**: Highlight quantifiable outcomes, metrics, lessons learned, and business value (20% of answer).

## Common Tech Behavioral Questions
1. "Tell me about a time you had a technical disagreement with a team member."
2. "Describe a project that failed or missed a deadline and what you learned."
3. "How do you handle tight project deadlines under shifting requirements?"
