# ATS Resume Guidelines & Optimization Strategies

## Overview of Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
Over 98% of Fortune 500 companies and tech organizations use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday to automatically screen resumes. An ATS parses CV text, categorizes skills, experience, and qualifications, and ranks candidates against job descriptions.

## Essential Formatting Guidelines
1. **Use Standard Document Headers**: Always include standard section titles such as "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Projects", and "Certifications". Non-standard titles like "Where I've Been" or "My Toolbox" confuse ATS parsers.
2. **File Formats**: Clean PDF files or standard .docx files are ideal. Ensure the PDF text is highlightable and selectable (not an image-based scan).
3. **Typography & Layout**: Avoid complex multi-column layouts, tables, text boxes, graphics, icons, or header/footer placements. Use clean single-column or clean dual-column layouts with standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Inter, Georgia).
4. **Keyword Matching**: Incorporate exact keyword matches from the target job posting. For instance, if the job description mentions "RESTful APIs", "Microservices", and "Python", ensure these exact phrases appear naturally in your experience bullet points.

## Quantifiable Impact & Metrics
ATS algorithms and hiring managers prioritize bullet points with measurable outcomes:
- **Formula**: *Accomplished X by implementing Y, resulting in Z.*
- **Example**: "Engineered an automated data pipeline using PySpark and Airflow, reducing batch processing latency by 45% and saving $12,000 monthly."
- Use strong action verbs: *Architected, Spearheaded, Optimized, Automated, Deployed, Reduced, Streamlined.*
